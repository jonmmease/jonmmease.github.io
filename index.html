Hi, I'm Jon Mease

I care deeply about the future of the open data science technology ecosystem, I’m a contributor to a variety of open source visualization and data science projects, and I'm the creator of VegaFusion.

I hold a Master’s Degree in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, and Bachelor's degrees in Mathematics and Physics from Millersville University.

For more information on my background, see my CV.

Featured Projects

VlConvert

VlConvert converts Vega-Lite and Vega chart specifications to a variety of static and interactive formats including SVG, PNG, PDF, HTML, and more. It also converts Vega-Lite to Vega.

VegaFusion

I created VegaFusion, which provides serverside acceleration of the Vega visualization grammar.

RAPIDS Datashader

I integrated RAPIDS technologies into the Datashader Python library to enable GPU acceleration.

Plotly Parallel Categories Chart

I developed the Plotly parallel categories (parcats) trace. This is an interactive chart type for visualizing multidimensional categorical datasets.
